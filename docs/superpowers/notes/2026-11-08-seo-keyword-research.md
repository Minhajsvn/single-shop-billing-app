# billo: SEO keyword and competitor research (UAE)

Date: 2026-11-08. Input for the landing page copy (step 2) and the page map (step 6).

**Limits of this research:** it comes from web search results, mostly vendor blogs and listing sites. There are
no search-volume numbers. Before finalising the copy, check volumes and difficulty in Google Keyword Planner
(set the location to United Arab Emirates), and again in Search Console four weeks after launch. Legal and tax
facts below must be checked on tax.gov.ae / mof.gov.ae before they go on the site.

## 1. Who we compete with in search

| Competitor | What they push | Takeaway for billo |
|---|---|---|
| Vyapar (vyapar.com/en-ae) | VAT billing, POS billing, FTA-ready reports. Has the most UAE SEO pages (`/pos-software`, invoicing, small business). Third-party listing shows about AED 320–650 a year. | The main rival for "billing software UAE". It's a general accounting app. billo wins on being a till: offline-first, cash register, close-day. |
| Zoho Invoice / Books | Free invoicing, VAT. | Not a shop counter: no barcode scanning, POS or stock at the till. billo can rank on "POS" terms Zoho doesn't target. |
| VasyERP, Managerium, BIMPOS, YESPOS, iBOS, Omega POS | Lots of "Top 10 POS software in UAE 2026" listicles. | These listicles rank well. Ask to be added to them (a backlink plus listicle traffic). |
| Hardware-bundle resellers (Neotech, Nastrum, Casio CS, RoyalPOS, Dubizzle listings) | Terminal plus software bundles for AED 1,000–10,000. Many say "lifetime offline software". | Shop owners compare against a one-off hardware cost. Say "works on the PC or tablet you already have" and give the monthly price. |

Gaps no competitor owns well:
- **Offline selling that syncs back.** Resellers say "offline" but mean desktop-only. billo is cloud *and* offline.
- **Cash register / close the day / cash variance.** Almost no UAE pages target this.
- **Plain-English, short how-to guides** on tax invoices for small shops. Existing ones are long accountant blogs.

## 3. Tax and compliance facts to write about (check before publishing)

- Simplified tax invoices are generally allowed for retail and B2C sales, and for B2B sales up to AED 10,000. A
  VAT-registered buyer can ask for a full tax invoice.
- The fields required on full and simplified invoices differ between sources. Use the FTA text, not blogs.
- **UAE e-invoicing (Peppol, ASP model):** per recent sources, a voluntary pilot from 1 July 2026; large
  businesses (AED 50M+ revenue) go live 1 January 2027; smaller businesses must appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027
  and go live 1 July 2027. One source says simplified invoices stop working once e-invoicing applies.
- **Do not claim billo is "e-invoicing ready".** It isn't yet (the ASP partner is an open owner item). Safe wording:
  "Your invoices already carry the TRN and VAT details. We're preparing for UAE e-invoicing ahead of the 2027
  deadline for small businesses."

The e-invoicing guide is the strongest content opportunity: every small shop has to act by 2027, and search
interest will rise through 2027.
